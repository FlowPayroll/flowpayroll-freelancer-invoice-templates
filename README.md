# FlowPayroll Freelance Invoice Templates

> An open collection of customizable invoices for freelancers and independent contractors. Choose a standard, hourly, printable, or USDT-focused format, fill in your information, and share it with a client—without creating an account.

Well-organized invoices make it easier for clients to understand what they owe and how to pay. This collection offers reusable formats for fixed-fee assignments, time-based services, and payments arranged in USDT on a specified blockchain network.

## Getting started

1. Select the invoice format that matches your agreement.
2. Make a copy and replace all `[placeholder]` entries with accurate billing and payment details.
3. Check the totals, applicable taxes, currency, due date, and payment instructions.
4. Export to PDF or print, send the invoice to your customer, and retain a copy for your records.

## Invoice information checklist

- **Supplier information:** Legal name or business, contact information, address, and any applicable tax identifier.
- **Customer information:** The person or organization being invoiced.
- **Reference and dates:** A unique invoice number, issue date, and payment deadline.
- **Services supplied:** Itemized descriptions, units or hours, agreed pricing, and amounts.
- **Amounts due:** Subtotal, relevant tax, total, and stated billing currency.
- **Settlement details:** The agreed bank instructions or cryptocurrency asset, network, and recipient wallet address.
- **Terms:** Payment timing, any agreed late fees, and other applicable terms.

## Handling USDT payment details

For a cryptocurrency settlement, both parties must confirm the **token and blockchain network** before sending funds. A wallet address alone may not identify which network should be used.

| USDT network | Common address format |
|---|---|
| TRC20 (TRON) | Typically begins with `T` |
| ERC20 (Ethereum) | Typically begins with `0x` |
| BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain) | Typically begins with `0x` |

**Important:** ERC20 and BEP20 addresses can look the same but belong to different networks. Transfers using the incorrect asset, chain, or recipient details may be irrecoverable. Verify compatibility with the receiving wallet or platform and consider a small test transfer.

The [USDT invoice](templates/usdt-crypto-invoice-template.md) includes dedicated fields for these details and for the transaction reference.
